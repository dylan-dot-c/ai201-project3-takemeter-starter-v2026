# Your label definitions, one per line, as `label: definition`.
# Keep these synchronized with the taxonomy in README.md.

analysis: makes a point about WRC or the game and backs it with a specific, checkable fact (a result, a stat, a timed comparison, or a test the writer ran) that is the reason given for the conclusion
hot_take: a confident claim about drivers, teams, rallies or the game with no support offered; any fact present is decoration, not the reason
reaction: an immediate personal response to something that just happened, on a stage the writer just drove or a rally they just watched; feeling, not argument, even if it contains a number
